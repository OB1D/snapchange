Constant-Difference Number Line

Bill and tender are joined by a rigid CHANGE GAP beam. Slide both markers together — the gap never changes.

Why it works: a − b = (a − k) − (b − k). Take the same amount k off both numbers and the difference is untouched. Pick k so the bill lands on a friendly ten and the change is instant.
